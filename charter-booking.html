A Magnificent Triumph Of Modern Administration

A Yacht Inquiry Shouldn't Take Eight Manual Steps

"Is a yacht available this Saturday for 12 people?" triggers respond, check availability, qualify, recommend, price, follow up, collect a deposit, confirm — mostly by hand.

Availability, pricing, and follow-up get juggled across WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook DMs, phone calls, and spreadsheets, with someone trying to remember who asked what.

By the time someone replies, the same guest has usually already messaged two or three other operators.

Whoever responds fastest, qualifies best, and follows up hardest wins the charter — and most operators have no system doing any of that automatically.
